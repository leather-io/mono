Bitcoin Staking (PoX-5) is live on Stacks, and you can stake directly from [app.leather.io/staking](/staking). It succeeds Stacking: lock your STX with a pool and earn rewards paid in sBTC every cycle.

## What's included

**Stacks staking pools**\
Compare providers side by side by rewards token, TVL and fee, then stake in a few clicks. Advanced users can bring their own signer manager.

**Your position, up front**\
If you're already staking, your position sits at the top of the page. Share or bookmark app.leather.io/staking/status to jump straight to your pool.

**Exit any time**\
Choose how long to stake (48 cycles by default) and leave whenever you need. The page shows when the next cycle closes, so a deadline never catches you off guard.

**Bitcoin staking pools**\
Added in September: lock sBTC in a Bitcoin bond through Esbee DAO, or join the Stacking DAO liquid bond waitlist. Staking a larger amount? Request institutional access from the same page.

Since launch we've added PlanBetter and Asymmetric Research as pool providers. Dual Stacking moved to Bitcoin Staking on August 24, 2026, so if you were dual stacking, pick a pool to keep earning.
