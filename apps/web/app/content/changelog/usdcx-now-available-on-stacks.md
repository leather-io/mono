USDCx, a USDC-backed stablecoin, is now live on Stacks—and fully supported in Leather.

USDCx is issued on Stacks and backed 1:1 by USDC held in Circle xReserve. It's interoperable with USDC across supported chains without relying on third-party bridges, giving you a trusted dollar-denominated asset for the Stacks ecosystem.

## What you can do with USDCx

**Hold and manage in Leather**\
View your USDCx balance alongside your other Stacks tokens in both the browser extension and mobile app.

**Use across Stacks DeFi**\
Swap, provide liquidity, or use USDCx as collateral in apps like Bitflow and Granite.

**Bridge from other chains**\
Bring USDC from supported networks to Stacks via the [Stacks bridge](https://bridge.stacks.co).

For more details on how USDCx works, check out the [Stacks onboarding documentation](https://docs.stacks.co).
