A round of Ledger improvements has landed in the extension over the past few releases.

**Verify receive addresses on your device**\
From the Receive screen, display your BTC or STX address on your Ledger and confirm it matches before you share it.

**A more reliable connection**\
Leather now connects to Ledger through Ledger's Device Management Kit, so connecting and signing on Bitcoin and Stacks is steadier.

**Ledger accounts at a glance**\
Ledger accounts carry a badge wherever you pick or approve an account.

Make sure your Ledger Stacks app is up to date. Leather now requires a recent version.
