See your full BTC and STX portfolio in one place

Knowing what you own shouldn't require mental math across multiple screens. Now you can see everything in one view. Check out the new features by connecting your wallet at [https://app.leather.io/portfolio](https://leather.io/portfolio)!

Connect your wallet at leather.io/portfolio to get a complete picture of your holdings—total value in USD, a breakdown of each token's share, and your recent activity.

## What's included

Total balance at a glance\
Your combined BTC and STX holdings displayed in USD. No more switching between tabs.

**Visual breakdown**\
A stacked bar chart shows how your portfolio is allocated across tokens. See which assets make up your holdings and how they compare.

**Token details**\
A sortable table lists each token with its current allocation percentage and balance. Find what you're looking for quickly.

**Recent activity**\
Your latest transactions in one feed. Track what's moving without digging through block explorers.

This is the first step toward a fuller portfolio experience in Leather. We'd love to hear what you want to see next.
