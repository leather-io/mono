If you've locked BTC in a Bitcoin Staking bond, the extension now counts it in your total balance and shows you where it is.

**Locked BTC, clearly labeled**\
Bonded BTC carries a lock chip on the Bitcoin row, appears in the Locked card, and gets its own "In a bond" row on All balances and the BTC details page.

**Bond positions page**\
See each bond with its amount, stacked STX, policy address, unlock date, status and rewards paid out. The page also shows the next registration window and a history of withdrawn and exited bonds.

**Sign bond exits**\
Bitcoin Staking apps can ask Leather to sign a bond exit, including from Ledger and multisig accounts.

Manage your bonds from the link on the positions page.
