Click any token in the extension to open its own details page.

**Token details**\
See your balance, its fiat value, market stats and recent activity for each token, with Send, Receive and Swap one click away.

**A head start for new wallets**\
Empty wallets now see a guide to getting their first token, plus short learn articles on sBTC, staking and getting started.
