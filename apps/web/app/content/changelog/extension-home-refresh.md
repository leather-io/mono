The extension home has been redesigned to put your balance and the actions you use most up front.

**New overview and actions**\
Your total balance sits at the top, with redesigned action buttons right below it.

**Tokens, Collectibles and Activity**\
Collectibles now have their own tab alongside Tokens and Activity.

**Cleaner token list**\
An updated token list design, plus a full Manage tokens page to choose exactly which tokens appear on your home screen.

**Reorganized settings**\
Settings are regrouped into clearer pages, with a network badge that shows which network you're on and a dedicated page for viewing your Secret Key.
