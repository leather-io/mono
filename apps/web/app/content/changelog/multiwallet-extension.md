You can now keep several wallets in Leather at once: each software wallet from its own Secret Key, plus as many Ledger wallets as you like, all unlocked with one password.

**Switch across wallets**\
The redesigned account picker groups accounts by wallet, so you can jump to any account in any wallet from one sheet.

**Add and manage wallets**\
Create, restore or connect a Ledger wallet from the same place. In Manage mode you can rename wallets and accounts, hide accounts you don't use, view a wallet's Secret Key or remove a wallet.

**Ledger at a glance**\
Ledger wallets and accounts carry a Ledger badge wherever you pick or approve an account, from the account switcher to connection and signing requests.
