Token details pages now open with a Price section: the live price, how much it's moved and a chart you can explore.

**Pick a period**\
Switch between one day, one week, one month, three months, six months and one year.

**Hover for history**\
Move across the chart to see the price and change at any point in time.

Newer tokens without enough history have the longer periods turned off, and tokens without a live price skip the section entirely.
