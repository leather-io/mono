We've rebuilt swaps in the extension, with clearer quotes, more control over each trade and support for Ledger.

**Quotes as you type**\
Pick your assets, enter an amount or set it to max, and see the estimated output right away. Flip the pair with one click.

**Know what you're signing**\
The review screen breaks down the rate, minimum received, price impact and every fee, with a tooltip on each line. You can adjust slippage right there.

**Powered by Bitflow**\
Leather gets swap quotes and routes from Bitflow.

**Ledger support**\
You can now swap from Ledger accounts and approve the trade on your device.
