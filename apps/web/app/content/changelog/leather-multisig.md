Multisig is now live at [app.leather.io/multisig](https://app.leather.io/multisig). Create a shared vault with your team, family or DAO, and require more than one signature before funds move on Bitcoin or Stacks.

Every member signs with the wallet they already use: a Leather extension account or a Ledger.

## What you can do

**Create a vault and invite members**\
Choose the members and how many signatures a transaction needs. Invite people by address or BNS name, and they join from their own wallet.

**Propose, sign and send**\
Send BTC, STX and SIP-10 tokens from a vault. One member proposes, the others review and sign in the extension, and the transaction goes out once enough members have signed. Set a custom fee when the network is busy.

**Balances and activity in one place**\
See each vault account's balances, pending proposals, who has signed and the full transaction history.

**Bring existing vaults with you**\
When a vault activates, Leather scans for accounts the same members already use on-chain, including Stacks vaults created with Asigna, and adds them automatically.

Apps can also ask the extension to propose a transaction from a multisig account instead of signing it alone.
