**USDCx now supported as a default asset**

USDCx balances now appear automatically in your wallet with accurate fiat pricing.

We've added USDCx to the default asset list and positioned it prominently so you can track your stable coin balance without any\
extra setup.

The wallet now loads USDCx balances alongside your other assets, complete with real-time price data.

One less token you need to manually add.
