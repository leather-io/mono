We've rebuilt Activity in the extension.

**Grouped by day, loads as you scroll**\
Transactions are grouped by date and load as you scroll. Pending transactions always stay under Today so they're easy to find.

**Swaps read like swaps**\
Swaps and sBTC bridge transactions show what you traded, not a generic contract call.

**Reclaim stuck sBTC deposits**\
Pending or failed sBTC deposits appear in your feed with a Reclaim link.

**Friendlier errors**\
If something fails to load, you'll see what went wrong and can retry while the rest of your feed stays visible.
